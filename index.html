About Me

Who I Am

I am Farhad Hossen, a Mechanical Engineering student at Shandong University of Technology (2026–2030), originally from Bagerhat, Khulna, Bangladesh. My journey blends academic rigor with hands-on curiosity — from physics and higher mathematics at SSC & HSC level to designing and simulating mechanical systems at university.

I focus on mechanical design optimization, manufacturing & automation, aerospace structures, and thermal & sustainable systems, while actively engaging with engineering clubs, workshops and learning communities.

What Drives Me

Engineering is about solving real problems with elegant, buildable solutions. I am driven to deepen my expertise in mechanical design and manufacturing, gain research exposure, and prepare for graduate study — all while growing through every course, project and challenge along the way.
